MMA head coach · Mikado Jiu Jitsu

Amjad Khan

Known as “Amjad the Wolf”, he has spent years in Pakistan’s cages and on its mats: Flogger Series and IFT Fight Pass in MMA, and a BJJ purple belt under Shaheryar Khalid. He has been at Mikado since the day it opened, and won gold at his first international competition in 2026.

The journey

From the cage to the podium

  1. 2018

    Into the gi

    Trains MMA, muay thai and grappling in Lahore, and starts Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in the gi as a white belt.

  2. 2020–21

    Flogger Series

    Takes MMA fights on the Flogger Series cards: at flyweight against Babar Ali in October 2020, and at bantamweight against Zabiullah Kohi in Rawalpindi in April 2021.

  3. 2021

    Gold in the gi

    Tops the podium at a PGC Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu tournament in December 2021, still a white belt.

  4. 2024

    Mikado from day one

    Is by Shaheryar Khalid’s side from the day Mikado Jiu Jitsu opens, and becomes the gym’s MMA head coach.

  5. 2025

    Back in the cage

    Fights Asad Warraich at IFT Fight Pass Lahore, going the full distance.

  6. 2026

    Gold in Kuala Lumpur

    His first international competition. In the Adult 145 division he faces much younger opponents and dangerous leg lockers, escapes the leg attacks and wins the final on points. He also beats a 110 kg opponent, and takes on the man who injured teammate Hashim Ali, “to finish what my brother started”.
