What you learn

Inside Adults Gi Jiu Jitsu

We don’t just teach techniques — we build understanding and problem-solving.

  • Start from zero

    Fundamentals is for all white belts from no stripe up to three stripes, focusing on the core techniques and foundations of Brazilian Jiu Jitsu.

  • Keep progressing

    Advance classes are open to all coloured belts and white belts with a minimum of four stripes.

  • Train around work

    An all-levels Gi class runs most weekday mornings, with Fundamentals and Advance in the evenings.

  • Compete if you want to

    Members compete for the gym at home and abroad, including at Grappling Industries Kuala Lumpur. It is never required.
