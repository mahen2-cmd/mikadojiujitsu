Our story

Built on Passion. United by Jiu-Jitsu.

Mikado Jiu Jitsu was founded in Lahore, Pakistan, in 2024 by Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt Shaheryar Khalid, following a lifelong journey shaped by discipline, perseverance, and a deep love for the art.

The journey began in 2011, when Shaheryar discovered Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) and began competing. As he gained experience, he found himself increasingly drawn to the grappling aspect of the sport. What started as one part of his MMA journey soon became a passion of its own, leading him to pursue Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu more deeply and dedicate himself to mastering the art.

Over the years, training, competing, and eventually coaching taught him that Jiu-Jitsu was about much more than fighting. It was about building character, developing resilience, understanding movement, and learning to overcome challenges both on and off the mats.

His journey eventually took him to Malaysia, where he continued developing as both an athlete and a coach under GFTeam, working alongside Professor Pedro Falbo. During his time there, Shaheryar coached students of different ages and skill levels, contributed to competition training, and continued competing while gaining valuable international experience. Living and working within the Malaysian Jiu-Jitsu community broadened his perspective on the art and strengthened his belief in the importance of thoughtful instruction, discipline, and continuous learning.

After years of dedication to the sport, Shaheryar earned his Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt under Professor Pedro Falbo in 2024, marking a significant milestone in his journey.

In December 2023, following the passing of his father, Shaheryar returned to Pakistan. During a difficult period in his life, he found himself reflecting on his future, his family, and the legacy he wanted to build. He decided to bring everything he had learned through years of training, competing, and coaching abroad back to his hometown of Lahore and establish a school of his own.

That decision led to the birth of Mikado Jiu Jitsu in 2024.

Mikado was founded on a simple vision: to create a place where people from all walks of life could learn, grow, and belong. More than just a training academy, Shaheryar wanted to build a community where Jiu-Jitsu could help people develop confidence, resilience, discipline, and a deeper understanding of themselves.

What started with just a handful of students grew into a community united by a shared commitment to learning, respect, and personal growth. From the beginning, Mikado was built on the belief that Jiu-Jitsu should be accessible to everyone, regardless of age, gender, background, or experience.

Today, Mikado Jiu Jitsu is more than a training academy. It is a community where children develop confidence, adults discover their potential, and athletes challenge themselves to become better every day. We believe in teaching the principles behind the techniques rather than simply repeating movements without understanding them. Our approach emphasizes conceptual learning, intelligent movement, and understanding why a technique works, rather than relying solely on drilling it thousands of times.

We value progress over shortcuts and believe that every achievement should be earned through consistent effort, patience, and dedication.

Our goal has never been simply to produce better fighters. It is to help build stronger, more confident, and more resilient people — on the mats and beyond them.

Mikado is built on the mats, strengthened by its community, and driven by the belief that everyone has the potential to grow.

This is our story. And we are only getting started.

Your first class

New to the mats?

Everything people ask before they step on for the first time.

I have never trained in any martial art. Can I join?

Absolutely! You don’t need any prior martial arts experience to join Mikado Jiu Jitsu. Our classes are designed to help beginners learn from the ground up in a safe, supportive, and welcoming environment. Our coaches will guide you through the fundamentals at your own pace, helping you build confidence, improve your fitness, and develop your skills step by step. Everyone starts somewhere, and we’re here to help you begin your journey.

How can I sign up for a class?

Simply contact us on WhatsApp at +92 331 4086323 or through social media to learn about our class schedules, membership options, and available programs. We’ll guide you through the registration process and help you find the right class based on your age, experience, and goals. Once you’re registered, you’ll be ready to step onto the mats and begin your Jiu-Jitsu journey with us!

How old do kids need to be?

At Mikado Jiu Jitsu, children can start training as young as 3 years old! Our kids’ programs are designed to suit different age groups and developmental stages, helping children build confidence, coordination, discipline, and resilience in a fun and supportive environment.

Do you have separate classes for women?

While we don’t currently offer separate women-only classes, women are welcome to train in our regular classes at Mikado Jiu Jitsu. We are committed to providing a safe, respectful, and supportive training environment where everyone feels comfortable learning and progressing at their own pace. Our coaches emphasize mutual respect, controlled training, and the well-being of every student, ensuring that women can develop their skills and confidence in a welcoming environment.

What is the difference between Gi and No Gi?

The main difference is the clothing worn and how techniques are applied. In Gi Jiu-Jitsu, practitioners wear a traditional uniform called a gi, which allows them to use grips on the collar, sleeves, and pants to control and submit their opponents.

In No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu, practitioners wear a rash guard and shorts without the traditional uniform. Gripping clothing is not allowed, so the focus shifts toward body control, underhooks, overhooks, and other forms of positional control.
