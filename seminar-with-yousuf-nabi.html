About Yousuf Nabi

An active 3rd Degree Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Black Belt, Yousuf Nabi is the founder of Elements Martial Arts and has dedicated his career to training, competing and developing Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu practitioners.

Yousuf was awarded his Black Belt by David Onuma in 2014, before receiving his 1st, 2nd and 3rd Degrees from Marco Canha. He has competed regularly throughout his career, including extensively at Black Belt level in both Gi and No-Gi competition.

He is also the owner of multiple martial arts academies, with multiple affiliations across the UK, and has spent many years building and developing the Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu community through coaching, competition and education.

During this seminar, Yousuf will share practical, competition-tested techniques and concepts developed through years of training, competing and coaching. The session will focus on effective techniques, strategic concepts and details that can be applied immediately by practitioners of all skill levels.
