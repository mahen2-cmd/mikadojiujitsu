Mikado family

Good people, no shortcuts

Mikado Jiu Jitsu is built by the people who keep showing up. Here are some of their stories, and every belt they’ve earned along the way.

Student spotlights

Meet the students

  • Spotlight · November 2025

    Mansoor Mujtaba

    Training for almost a year and a half

    Mansoor has been showing up, putting in the work, and making time for his fitness through jiu-jitsu — no excuses, no shortcuts. His consistency, discipline and quiet dedication make him one of the standout students at Mikado.

  • Spotlight · November 2025

    Hashim Ali

    Now one of Mikado’s kids coaches

    Some students train hard — others train with heart. Hashim shows up early, stays late, and never complains, no matter how tough the rolls get. In August 2026 he fought through an injury to win gold at Grappling Industries Kuala Lumpur.

  • Spotlight · October 2025

    Eshal

    Training for almost a year

    Eshal brings an incredible amount of energy, enthusiasm and positivity to every class. Whether it’s drills, games or sparring, she gives it her all with a smile on her face — and her spirit lifts everyone around her.

  • Spotlight · October 2025

    Suleman & Zargham

    The first two kids to join Mikado

    From day one, Suleman and Zargham have been side by side on the mats. Both now grey belts, they’ve pushed each other, learned together, and grown into amazing training partners and friends.

  • Spotlight · September 2025

    Abdul Wahab

    From his first day to now

    Abdul Wahab’s journey has been nothing short of inspiring. His consistency, discipline and hunger to improve make him stand out every single session — and this is just the beginning of his story.

  • Spotlight · August 2025

    Adil Tirmizey

    Full-time lawyer

    A full-time lawyer who still makes time to put in the work on the mats. Balancing the courtroom and the mats, Adil shows that discipline and consistency can make anything possible.
