What you learn

Inside No Gi Grappling

  • Leg locks

    Leg entanglements and the leg-lock game that modern No Gi competition is built on.

  • Wrestling

    Takedowns and scrambles worked into your Jiu Jitsu, so you can start the fight standing.

  • Back takes

    Getting to the back and finishing from there, against partners who aren’t wearing a gi to grip.

  • At your level

    Fundamentals for newer grapplers, and Advance for coloured belts and white belts with four stripes.
