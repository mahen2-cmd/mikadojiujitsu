What they learn

More than takedowns and guard passes

Jiu Jitsu gives kids real self-defence skills — but more importantly, it gives them character.

  • Confidence

    Shy kids find their voice. Real confidence comes from doing hard things — and doing them again.

  • Control and focus

    High-energy kids learn control. Every class follows the same clear flow: warm-up, technique, drills, then rolling.

  • Resilience

    On the mat, it is okay to struggle, to lose and to make mistakes. What matters is showing up again.

  • Respect

    Respect for coaches and training partners is part of the culture, and it goes home with them.

  • Real self-defence

    How to fall, get back up and stay calm under pressure — so they can stand up for themselves without fear.

Competing

Mikado kids on the podium

In August 2026 our young athletes stepped onto the international stage at Grappling Industries Kuala Lumpur, competing against kids from academies across the region and bringing home wins and medals.

Zargham, Shaheryar’s very first student, became the first Mikado kid to compete for the gym. Competing is never required — but the door is open for every kid who wants to test themselves.
